Hi! I am **Serjin Kim**, a first-year Master's student in Finance at [MIT Sloan School of Management](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/). Before MIT, I studied economics at [Seoul National University (SNU)](https://en.snu.ac.kr/). At SNU, I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Sanghack Lee](https://www.sanghacklee.me) on causal machine learning and [Prof. Jaewon Choi](https://sites.google.com/site/jaewchoi1203) on corporate finance.

I consider myself both an econ and a tech person, and I'm fascinated by how the two shape and advance each other. My research interests center on financial markets, from using econometrics and modern machine learning/deep learning to understand them to exploring how emerging technologies reshape their dynamics.

Outside of research, I enjoy baking, exploring art exhibitions, and playing tennis. I'm also a competitive kendo athlete.

I'm always exploring new research questions and directions, and I love exchanging ideas. I'm always up for a coffee! Feel free to reach out at [serjin@mit.edu](mailto:serjin@mit.edu).
